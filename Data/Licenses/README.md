# Additional dictionary data

These licenses apply to the corresponding generated `Data/<locale>.lua` word lists
derived from the [LibreOffice dictionary collection](https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/32b006a2c22a4ac7e8ed3f03346f7b3d85a970a4).

- enGB: British English dictionary by Kevin Atkinson, David Bartlett, Brian Kelk,
  Andrew Brown, Marco A.G.Pinto and contributors; LGPL (see upstream notice).
- deDE: igerman98/frami by Bjoern Jacke and Franz Michael Baumann; GPL v2 or v3.
- frFR: Grammalecte by Olivier R. and contributors; MPL 2.0.
- esES: RLA-ES; GPL v3+, LGPL v3+, or MPL 1.1+ (upstream alternatives).
- itIT: LibreItalia / Marina Latini and the authors named in the legacy notices; GPL v3.
- nlNL and nlBE: Stichting OpenTaal and contributors; BSD 3-clause or CC BY 3.0.

Modified for MisspelledForever on 2026-10-05: expanded finite standalone inflections,
filtered to Latin words of 2-28 characters, normalized accents and apostrophes,
converted to lowercase and retained no-suggestion flags. Compound synthesis is not
included. Original notices and license texts are preserved in the locale folders.
The original English (US) data remains covered by `DICTIONARY-LICENSE.txt`.
